INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The controlled benchmark indicates that behavior-preservation verification is the primary contributor to the reduction in false acceptance of LLM-generated patches.

The visible + hidden test baseline produced 24 false acceptances, while VERA-SE produced 0.

The structural scope constraint did not reduce false acceptance in this benchmark and introduced one false rejection, showing a conservative precision-recall tradeoff.

LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- The benchmark currently contains six synthetic repair tasks.
- The 240 candidates were generated from repeated runs over these tasks, so candidate count should not be interpreted as 240 independent bugs.
- Behavior probes were manually specified for the controlled tasks.
- Results should therefore be treated as controlled prototype evidence, not final evidence of generalization to real-world software repositories.
